About MEOW
Official Guild Story • Est. 2026

Built by Friends. Driven by Pure Fun.

Welcome to MEOW — a Free Fire gaming community created with one simple belief: gaming is always better when you play with people who feel like family. No unnecessary pressure, no toxic egos — just good vibes, great company, and unforgettable matches.

How It All Started

MEOW wasn't born out of a desire to build a strict, high-pressure esports organization. It started simply as a hangout space for friends — a place to laugh, relax, team up in Free Fire, explore Craftland, or jump into any game together after a long day. Over time, that small circle of friends grew into an official guild where everyone belongs.

Why the Name "MEOW"?

In a gaming world full of overly serious and repetitive clan names, we wanted something that stood out immediately. MEOW felt unique, playful, and effortlessly memorable — a name that reflects our fun-loving spirit while keeping our identity completely original.

What Makes Us Different

Most guilds judge players by their K/D ratio, rank badges, or stats. At MEOW, we care about the person behind the screen. You don't need to be a pro player to wear our tag — if you have good behavior, respect for others, and a friendly mindset to mingle with everyone, our doors are always open for you.

What We Do Together

Every day at MEOW is about making memories with the squad. Here is how we spend our time:

Craftland Fun Maps Exploring crazy, creative, and hilarious custom Craftland maps together for nonstop entertainment.
Friendly Custom Rooms Hosting chill, friendly custom matches where winning is fun, but laughing with friends matters more.
Discord Hangouts Late-night voice chats, music, jokes, and casual hangouts on our official MEOW Discord server.

Friends By Chance. Squad By Choice.

Whether you're here for Craftland adventures, friendly custom rooms, or just a chill place to talk and make real friends, you'll always have a spot in MEOW.

MEOW Affiliates
Community Partners & Creators

Growing Together Through Friendship.

At MEOW, we love teaming up with friendly guilds, creative Craftland map makers, and passionate gaming creators who share our chill, zero-toxicity vibe.

Partner Guilds

We are always open to building friendly alliances with other Free Fire guilds for fun custom rooms, Craftland showdowns, and community hangouts. Our official partner roster is currently open for friendly collaborations.

Official Creators & Map Makers

From gameplay editors and content creators to talented Craftland map builders, we love supporting our community's creative side:

Guild Creators & Editors Explore the YouTube, Instagram, and social profiles of our talented members in our VIP Spotlight.
Craftland Map Creators Play fun, creative custom maps shared by our squad and friends inside the Craftland Hub.

Want to Collaborate With MEOW?

Have a friendly guild, Discord server, or gaming channel? Reach out to our team on Discord or via Support to plan friendly custom matches or community collaborations.

MEOW Careers
Guild Staff & Volunteer Roles

Help Us Build a Better Community.

MEOW runs on friendship, teamwork, and passion. If you enjoy helping others, hosting fun matches, or creating cool gaming content, you can step up and take an official role in our guild team.

Open Community Roles

We are currently welcoming friendly, active members for the following volunteer positions:

Elder Council / Support Officer Assist guild members, handle support tickets on our website, and help keep our community friendly and well-organized.
Discord Moderator Welcome new members, keep voice and text channels lively, and ensure zero toxicity across the official MEOW Discord server.
Event & Custom Room Host Organize chill friendly custom rooms, Craftland fun map sessions, and weekend squad hangouts for everyone to enjoy.
Content Creator & Editor Capture funny squad moments, edit gameplay clips, and represent MEOW on YouTube, Instagram, or other platforms.

Important Note

All positions listed above are voluntary community roles (unpaid). We don't look for strict professional resumes — good behavior, honesty, and a genuine love for hanging out with the squad are all you need.

Ready to Step Up?

Interested in any of these roles? Reach out to an On-Duty Elder through our Support desk and mention which role you'd like to help with.

Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 2026 • MEOW Official HQ

Your Privacy & Data Safety.

Welcome to the official website of MEOW Free Fire Guild. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and gaming information you share with our community. This Privacy Policy explains in detail what data we collect, how our website features use it, how it is stored, and what rights you have over your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you apply to join MEOW, activate your profile, or use features on our platform, we collect only the information necessary to run our guild community smoothly:

Guild Application & Profile Details Real Full Name, In-Game Name (IGN), Free Fire Game UID, Date of Birth (used to auto-calculate Age), Gender, Playing Style, Hobby, and Address (Street, City, District, State, and 6-digit PIN Code).
Account Credentials & Guild Status System-generated Application ID (e.g., MG-26-XXXX), login password, guild role (Member, Elder, Leader), prestige badge tier, and join date.
Activity, Check-In & Event Data Daily Present (attendance) calendar history, login streaks, virtual guild coins, and Lucky Spin ticket records (ticket tier, ticket number, and lucky numbers).
VIP Spotlight & Support Records Public social media links you voluntarily add to VIP Spotlight (up to 5 links), support tickets sent to On-Duty Elders, and moderation/disciplinary records if any rule violation occurs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for guild management and community fun — never for commercial advertising or spam. Specifically, your information is used to:

Verification & Roster Display Verify authentic members, prevent duplicate UID registrations, and display active squad members in the official Roster.
Community Features & Support Power your personal profile dashboard, generate official A4 PDF Player Profiles and Cancellation Certificates, track Daily Present rewards, run Lucky Spin events, and process Support tickets.

3. Camera & Gallery Access (QR Code Login)

Our website includes a built-in QR Code scanner (powered by html5-qrcode) to help you log in quickly using your MEOW ID Card. Camera and gallery permissions are requested only when you tap the QR scanner icon. All scanning happens locally on your device — we never capture, record, upload, or store your camera feed or personal gallery photos on any server.

4. Data Storage, LocalStorage & Third-Party APIs

To keep the website fast, interactive, and accurate, we rely on the following secure services:

Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) Core guild records (profiles, applications, notices, support tickets, Craftland maps, and VIP Spotlight links) are securely stored in our Firebase database.
Postal PIN Code Verification API During First-Time Profile Setup, your entered city and 6-digit PIN code are checked against the India Postal PIN Code API (api.postalpincode.in) solely to verify location accuracy.
Browser LocalStorage & SessionStorage Your login activity graph data, read/unread Notice Board alerts, and popup display preferences are stored locally in your own browser to improve your experience.
On-Device PDF Generation When you download your Official Player Profile or Cancellation Certificate PDF, the document is generated directly on your device using html2pdf.js.

5. Data Sharing & Public Visibility

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Only your gaming identity (IGN, UID, Guild Role, Badge, and voluntary VIP Spotlight links) is visible to other visitors on the Roster and Spotlight pages. Sensitive details such as passwords and administrative logs remain strictly restricted to authorized Guild Command.

6. Data Retention & Auto-Cleanup

We keep temporary data clean through automated schedules built into our system:

Notice Board & Support Tickets Official HQ Notices are automatically cleared after 7 days. Support tickets marked as "Solved" are automatically removed after 2 days.
Account Cancellation & Deletion You can request profile removal anytime using the Cancel Registration form. Once approved and confirmed, active accounts follow a 3-day countdown (and pending accounts follow a 1-day countdown) before being removed from the active roster. Basic revoked records are kept in our administrative archive to prevent misuse.

7. Gaming Disclaimer & Community Safety

MEOW is an independent, fan-run Free Fire community guild and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garena International. Please never share your real Free Fire account password, OTP, or financial details with anyone — MEOW Command will never ask for them.

8. Policy Updates

As we add new features to the MEOW HQ website, we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any major changes will be announced on our official Notice Board and Discord server.

Have Privacy Questions or Data Requests?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, want to update your details, or need help removing your data, contact us anytime via Email or our official Discord server.

Terms of Service
Effective Date: 2026 • MEOW Official HQ

Terms of Service.

Welcome to MEOW Official Free Fire Guild. By accessing our website, submitting a guild application, or using any of our community features (such as Daily Present, Lucky Spin, VIP Spotlight, or Support Desk), you agree to follow these Terms of Service.

1. Eligibility & Open Membership

MEOW is a friendly, fun-focused gaming community open to everyone regardless of age, rank, or in-game stats. We do not enforce strict competitive requirements — good behavior, honesty, and a friendly mindset to mingle with the squad are all you need to be part of MEOW.

2. Registration & Account Security

To keep our guild database clean and authentic, every member must follow these account rules:

Authentic Details & Unique UID You must provide accurate personal details, a valid 6-digit PIN code location, and your real Free Fire Game UID. Duplicate UID registrations are not permitted.
Credential Safety You are responsible for keeping your system-generated Application ID (e.g., MG-26-XXXX), login password, and QR ID Card secure. Never share your credentials with others.

3. Community Code of Conduct

Every member of MEOW is bound by our core Guild Rules both in-game and on our platforms:

Zero Toxicity Protocol Respect all members and opponents at all times. Abusive language, harassment, ego-clashing, or discrimination will result in immediate disciplinary action.
Activity & Leave Notice Try to stay active with the squad. Unexcused inactivity for more than 3 consecutive days may lead to slot revocation. If you need a break, simply inform an On-Duty Elder through the Support Desk.
Discord Participation Important announcements, custom room details, and Craftland hangouts are coordinated through our official MEOW Discord server.

4. Virtual Coins, Daily Present & Lucky Spin

Our website includes fun community reward features governed by the following terms:

No Real-Money Value Virtual guild coins earned through Daily Present (50 coins/day and 1,000 bonus coins on a 7-day streak) and Exclusive Shop tickets (Rare, Epic, Mythic) are strictly for fun community events. They hold zero real-world monetary value, cannot be exchanged for cash, and are non-transferable.
Ticket Limits & Expiry Members can hold only 1 active ticket at a time. Tickets automatically expire once the scheduled Lucky Spin event timeline ends, and consumed tickets cannot be refunded.

5. VIP Spotlight, Craftland Hub & Support Desk

When using our interactive community sections, you agree to use them responsibly:

VIP Spotlight Links Members may add up to 5 personal social media links. Posting spam, phishing URLs, adult (18+) content, or harmful links is strictly prohibited and will lead to link removal or account ban.
Support Desk & Craftland Support tickets require a verified Application ID and should only be used for genuine guild queries. Tickets marked as "Solved" are automatically removed after 2 days.

6. Moderation, Suspension & Cancellation

Guild Leaders and authorized Elders reserve the right to temporarily suspend or permanently block any account that violates these Terms or disrupts the community. Members may also voluntarily leave at any time using the Cancel Registration form (3-day deletion countdown for active profiles; 1-day countdown for pending accounts). Official PDF profiles and cancellation certificates are for guild record purposes only.

7. Official Disclaimer

MEOW is an independent, fan-run Free Fire community guild and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or officially endorsed by Garena International. All game trademarks, logos, and assets belong to their respective owners. We reserve the right to update these Terms as new features are added to MEOW HQ.

Questions About Our Terms?

If you need clarification on any rule or want to report a violation, feel free to contact Guild Command via Email or our official Discord server.
