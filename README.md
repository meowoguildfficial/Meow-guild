## How to Join

1. Go to [https://meowoguildfficial.github.io/Meow-guild/](https://meowoguildfficial.github.io/Meow-guild/).
2. Open **Application Form** and choose **Join Guild**.
3. Enter your In-Game Name (IGN), Free Fire UID, playing style, and a valid Gmail address.
4. After review by the Leader or Elders, your Application ID and login credentials are sent directly to your Gmail.
